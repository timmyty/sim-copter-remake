# Gameplay options and population — October 14, 2026

User-requested departures from original behavior, implemented in the shared Unreal project:

- **Updated October 15:** click **R3** for persistent Tools; a second click switches to Dispatch and further clicks alternate. Releasing R3 never confirms or closes either menu. A confirms the last highlight (including after recentering); B cancels. X remains an alternate page switch. R3 map/help/radio shortcuts still use the physical hold and cancel the wheel before acting. Camera adjustment uses held X; X+A toggles the spotlight. See [controller click behavior](controller-radial-click.md).
- Settings → Graphics → Interface → **Cockpit Decorations: Off** collapses the whole decorative frame: side wiring, bottom sill and retract button. Dashboard, map and functional tool flaps remain. Settings cancellation restores the entered value; normal config persistence saves it.
- Building-window lighting follows the evaluated primary atmospheric sun's elevation (+5° to −3° smooth fade), with the clock curve retained as fallback. The world subsystem ticks while paused, including Photo Mode. Keeping the day/night edge across time seeks prevents continuous nightly window-pattern rerolls.
- A locally controlled aircraft with 0–10% fuel plays the low-fuel cue immediately and every 20 simulation seconds. Refueling above 10% resets the warning interval; empty fuel stops reminders.
- Water impacts now douse the burning-debris pool and credit its owning mission exactly once. Existing debris burnout still resolves through expiration. Fire markers require an active flame, burning vehicle/wreck or burning debris owned by that event; old event coordinates alone cannot produce a fire marker.
- Removed pilot health text. Unprotected dry falls of at least 9 m cause an injury/audio response; at least 18 m causes death and the existing five-second recovery. Minor falls do not accumulate hidden health damage. Water and a fully slowed parachute landing remain safe.
- **Remote Taser** is a $2,500 fleet equipment purchase (new bit `0x80`, enum appended to preserve saved ordinals). The mount, panel and radial entry are absent without ownership. Legacy saves do not acquire it for free. Purchase, sale, aircraft transfer and runtime save paths use the existing equipment mask.
- Apache purchase price is 1.5× the most expensive civilian helicopter. The catalog unlocks at the user city's 3,000-point progression tier or through **Unlock Apache**; the cheat reveals it for purchase. Existing discovery/ownership routes remain. The cheat reuses the saved encounter-unlock flag.
- Service crews and scripted performers no longer consume the civilian population budget. The original leading-edge scan is supplemented by at most two civilian refill attempts per spawn think, including while stationary. The refill respects original terrain/class rules, minimum distance and civilian/total actor caps. It samples the original eight-tile pedestrian neighborhood; using the much larger vehicle radius caused the next twelve-tile population cull to remove new people, which the all-map tests exposed and now prevent.
- The faded pause overlay now reads only **PAUSED**; the “START RESUME” line is removed.

Population regression coverage is map-discovered, not Heven-specific: `SimCopter.Population.UserCities` enumerates the same files as Start User Game. `-SimPopulationCities="<installation>/SimCopter/cities"` includes the installed city browser and automatically covers newly added maps. Each case loads actual SC2 spawn data, checks that posted police/medics do not consume civilian slots, samples appearances across the map, and separately exercises stationary refill and retention after culling. Whole-map sampling is shuffled deterministically; local refill starts with a fresh pool so normal distance despawning does not invalidate the assertion. Maps with no eligible civilian ground must not force people onto unsuitable terrain. All 16 installed user maps passed, with 5–11 civilian appearances and 16 retained new civilians over eight stationary updates per map.
