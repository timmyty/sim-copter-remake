# SimCopter Remake v1.0.19

A playtest-driven fix release. Thanks to **Daimo** (Discord) for playtesting.

## Gameplay

- **People no longer run backwards.** Car dodges, guided walks, curiosity visits and
  escorts turned the figure directly; when they handed control back, the stored facing
  was unchanged, so the figure kept the old yaw while walking along the new one. The
  facing is now re-applied whenever it drifts, and dodging people turn instantly.
- **Medics dropped beside a patient find them.** The onlooker crowd around a casualty no
  longer blocks route planning, a nearby patient falls back to the original straight
  approach when no checked route exists, and a much closer responder can take over a
  patient reserved by a distant one.
- **Police find a burglar who has left the car.** While the burglar is out of sight, the
  officer stakes out the stopped getaway car; reaching it completes the arrest. A visible
  burglar is still pursued first.
- **More frequent missions.** After a mission is completed, the next scheduled mission is
  due within 10 seconds (the difficulty-based concurrency limit still applies).
- **Fire engines go home.** A fire truck with nothing burning within range returns to its
  station after about 8 seconds instead of waiting out its 3-minute stay.
- **No more car piles beside the hangar (all maps).** Ambient cars are not spawned beside
  a parked aircraft or at the tail of a stopped queue, and ordinary ambient cars stuck for
  12 s (out of view) or 40 s (in view) are recycled. Mission, emergency, jammed and
  burning cars are never recycled.
- **Lighter loads.** Bucket water slows the helicopter 75% less than before, and a car or
  boat in the capture cage now slows it by the same amount as a full bucket.
- **Taller fire stations** (FS211, 1.35x) to match the police station and hospital.

## Presentation

- **Light cloud cover on city tours**, except maps whose land is mostly sandy/bare ground.
  Tunable with `SimCopter.Tour.CloudCoverage` and `SimCopter.Tour.DesertFraction`.
- **Night windows no longer blink on and off.** Every building origin landed exactly on the
  window hash's rounding boundary; a sub-centimetre nudge keeps the per-building roll stable.
  The nightly re-roll also has hysteresis.
- **Black patches on buildings.** Runtime-built building meshes now carry Lumen card data,
  and the back-facing copy of each two-sided face sits 2.5 mm behind the front copy, so
  hardware Lumen rays no longer land on a back face and return black.
- **Capture cage lighting.** The cage, its skid rails and the recovery-site props were built
  inside out (the far inner faces were drawn), which made them look black and lit from
  the wrong side. The bars are also a lighter steel.
- **Rescue camera with the cage deployed.** The bars are hidden from the pilot's own Rescue
  view, whose eye sits inside the cage; other views still show them.
- **Hangar exterior shimmer.** The hangar shell and frosted glazing follow the sun model
  only, removing a frame-to-frame feedback loop with the measured scene brightness.
- **Quieter city starts.** Civilian Simlish chatter waits 25 seconds after a city starts and
  is then limited to one line every 2.5 seconds city-wide.

## Controls and tools

- **Catalog History and Description panels are controller-selectable.** Highlight a panel
  with the D-pad and scroll it with D-pad Up/Down or the right stick; at the top or bottom
  the D-pad moves on to the next control.
- **One diagnostics launcher.** `Launch-With-Diagnostics.cmd` replaces the separate GPU and
  performance launchers and records GPU crash evidence and performance traces together
  (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\SimCopterRemake\Saved\Logs\Diagnostics.log` and `Profiling\`).

## Second playtest round

- **Capture cage view:** the Rescue-view change is reverted; the cage bars and skid rails
  are now hidden only from the pilot's own first-person (cockpit) view.
- **Harbor and recovery drops:** a boat or car dropped from a normal hover over the ring is
  accepted (up to 9 m above it, slower than 3.5 m/s). Acceptance is announced in the
  mission log with a chime: "HARBOR accepted the boat. Recovery complete." A drop that does
  not count says why (slow down, or lower the cage).
- **Mission variety:** fires get at least Transport's share wherever fires are enabled, and
  Transport is capped at a third of enabled jobs; two Transports in a row get one re-roll.
  Custom cities offer fires from the first stage when the aircraft carries water equipment.
- **Transport locations:** new fares choose among several nearby landing clearings, avoiding
  ones recent fares used, for both pickup and drop-off (career and user maps). Saved fares
  keep their destinations.
- **Police and burglars:** police on foot or in a police car next to a burglar's car stop it;
  an officer arrests the burglar at the car. About a third of the time the burglar bails out
  and runs, and the officer chases and arrests them. Street officers return to their car.
- **Medics:** a medic only loses a patient to another responder who is actually nearby and
  free (not riding in an ambulance or cabin, not carrying someone else); a blocked step keeps
  trying round the obstacle for 12 seconds; knocked-down casualties count as patients; a
  patient at exactly eight tiles is no longer reserved and then refused.
- **Hangar:** the outside bay posts no longer share the wall's plane (no flicker). An aircraft
  left parked inside the hangar when the pilot gets out returns to its home pad.
- **Catalog:** B in Upgrades returns to the helicopter list; B again closes the catalog.
- **Thin parts too dark (radar dish, signs):** the back side of each two-sided face is now lit
  as a back side.
- **Tour clouds:** the override retries until the cloud layer exists, follows the live cloud
  material after the 1 PM time is applied, keeps the layer on in Low Power, and only maps that
  are at least 70% sandy ground stay clear.
- **City Hall:** the entry hedges no longer straddle the grey podium wall or share planes with
  each other, and faces looking back toward the building (the hedge by the door) are lit
  correctly instead of black.
- **Police and fire stations:** both are the hospital's height; their beacons change colour every
  0.3 s instead of every 50 ms; their flat upper roofs are light concrete.
- **Docks:** AP223 piers are rebuilt as timber docks: plank deck with gaps, stringers, rails and
  pilings that run down into the water.
