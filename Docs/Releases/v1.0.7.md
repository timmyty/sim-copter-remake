# SimCopter Remake v1.0.7 - traffic, flight and rescue update

This Windows release includes all completed gameplay changes since v1.0.6.

## What's new

- **Smarter traffic:** cars follow the correct lane and road height, stop instead of creeping into queues, and choose less congested intersection exits. Cars stuck behind one another can briefly phase through other ambient cars. Aircraft, red lights, disabled vehicles and mission traffic retain their stops.
- **Helper progression and flight speed:** automatic mission helpers unlock only after graduating the current city, including when entering a city through a career warp. Helpers cruise more slowly. Without the speed cheat, holding either Shift makes horizontal helicopter travel 30% faster and tilts the helicopter more steeply. Existing cheat turbo speed stays unchanged.
- **Remote helicopter taser:** every helicopter has a visible nose-mounted taser and AIM TASER control, without needing a police passenger. The automatic pilot follows a targeted robber or criminal car while the player aims through a full horizontal turn and up/down to 85 degrees. Hitting a car disables it for eight seconds; repeat hits refresh the stop, and saved games retain the remaining time.
- **Automatic landing and pilot recovery:** exiting an airborne helicopter clears held controls and briefly holds position, then searches for a nearby safe landing within 18 metres. A purchased harness can recover a carried person and then the pilot on uneven ground. Obstructions cause a new search or a safe wait. Dropped people detach correctly and stay down until the pilot walks away and returns. Damaging falls have pain sounds, with an extra cue for severe falls.
- **Medical response:** patient markers follow the actual casualty, including elevation and relocation. Deployed medics reserve separate patients and route around physical obstacles. One hospital medic can board as standby without an active medical call.
- **Capture cage and transport:** purchased cages have a matching cockpit panel, held lowering/raising controls, visible skid fittings, forming bars and an automatic floor seal. Cage ownership increases crime mission availability. Transport jobs use nearby clear landing areas; helpers guide passengers to pickups and finish loaded deliveries before recall.
- **Money cheat odds:** failure chances are 2% for amounts 1-16,666, 4% for 16,667-33,333 and 7% for 33,334-49,999.
- **Wind turbines:** collisions follow the actual rotating blades, leaving the gaps between them clear.

These are deliberate additions to the remake. Existing NPC appearance, original city flyover, movie, radio and other v1.0.6 improvements remain included. See [the complete change log](../ForkChanges.md) and [controls](../AirOperations.md).

## Download and run

Download `SimCopterRemake-v1.0.7-Windows-x64.zip`, extract the entire archive, and run `SimCopterRemake.exe`. Keep the included folders beside the launcher. Unreal Editor is not required. If Windows requests the Visual C++ runtime, use `Engine/Extras/Redist/en-us/vc_redist.x64.exe`.

The package contains 45 maps, 21 KINV tracks, both drive-in movies and 30 city flyover soundtracks. Personal saves, settings, logs and debug symbols are excluded. `BUILD_INFO.json` records the source revision and executable hash; the separate `SHA256SUMS.txt` verifies the download.
