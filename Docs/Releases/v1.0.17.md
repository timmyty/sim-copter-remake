# SimCopter Remake v1.0.17 — water towers and city lettering

Water towers now have painted ivory tanks, blue trim, a thin brass stripe, metal
seams, rivets and subtle weathering. Large city-name lettering wraps onto opposite
sides: original career maps use their official city names, and imported maps use
their own names.

The update preserves tower geometry, collision and instancing. All towers in a city
share one material and one small mipmapped lettering texture, generated only while
the city loads. It adds no per-frame tower work or separate text objects.

The existing controller-menu fixes and confirmed external city-download links are retained.

## Downloads

- **SimCopterRemake-v1.0.17-Windows-x64.7z** — complete Windows game. Extract the entire
  archive and run `SimCopterRemake.exe`.
- **SimCopterRemake-v1.0.17-Optional-Development-Windows-x64.7z** — matching optional
  GPU/performance diagnostics. Extract its contents into the complete game folder.
- **SHA256SUMS.txt** — checksums for both archives.

Existing optional music packs remain compatible. Save formats are unchanged.
