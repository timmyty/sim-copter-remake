# SimCopter Remake v1.0.8 — hangar, capture cage and fleet update

This Windows release gathers the completed work since v1.0.7, including the
revamped hangar, vehicle recovery, fleet restoration and controller improvements.
The README now shows the hangar, a car secured in the capture cage, and the Cheat
Codes menu and guide.

## What's new

- **Rebuilt hangar and equipment previews.** The airport workshop now has tall
  gridded windows, timber/skylight roof, steel trusses, rear office/stairs, lamps,
  caution signs and correctly scaled furniture and display aircraft. The shop's
  equipment page identifies the active helicopter, browses models, marks owned
  fittings and previews all six equipment types. Mouseover no longer replaces a
  clicked selection; controller focus remains inside the hangar menu.
- **A fleet that survives graduation.** The next airport restores every missing
  owned helicopter, including previously affected saves. Existing fuel, damage,
  position, player selection and money are preserved. Equipment purchases apply
  to the owned fleet and later deliveries without refilling existing ammunition.
  Sold types and helper aircraft do not become free deliveries.
- **Capture cage for vehicles and boats.** The cage replaces the tow clamp,
  encloses stalled/fleeing/taser-disabled cars or rescued boat hulls, and seals
  its floor automatically. The lower rim fits the cargo; raising an occupied
  cage keeps it deployed. Junkyard, harbor and police-roof drops use actual
  footprint, height and speed checks. Destination markers follow the cargo's
  needs, and the helper uses the same recovery rules. Old clamp ownership and
  saved cargo migrate to the cage.
- **Police tools and custody.** A redesigned handheld energy taser charges on a
  visible active criminal, launches a swept homing discharge and adds dedicated
  sounds. Remote aim turns freely, follows targets within normal boosted flight
  limits, and keeps its shot/impact effects visible. Vehicle stuns last 16 seconds.
  Officers physically retrieve stunned suspects, and arrested/caged suspects do
  not keep generating escape warnings or penalties while awaiting delivery.
- **Pilot landing and harness recovery.** Unattended landing waits for the falling
  pilot and selects a reachable surface near the grounded pilot, avoiding roofs
  overhead. Purchased rescue harnesses handle unsuitable landing ground and can
  accept a carried person automatically as the pilot walks into reach. Capacity
  and obstruction checks preserve the patient until a safe handoff is possible.
- **Medical, mission and damage fixes.** Deployed medics traverse steep hills,
  coordinate patient claims and report empty searches. Injured medics release
  their patients. Repair, fuel and tear-gas service requests create spaced crews.
  Rooftop people fall with collapsed buildings; blasts can injure and throw them
  without granting rewards for newly caused injuries. Recovery records no longer
  take original scheduler slots, and stalled-car work yields to original jobs.
- **Controller and movement updates.** X opens Tools/Dispatch, Y boards/exits,
  A confirms, and L3 toggles Turbo Mode. Tools includes Remote Taser/Fly Helicopter
  and Passengers; Dispatch includes Recall All. Stick-neutral retains the selected
  option. Map chords zoom, choose missions and toggle markers without operating
  other tools or changing turbo. LB/RB steering no longer latches off after their
  retired wheel shortcut. On-foot camera pitch reaches ±85 degrees.
- **Airport celebration stays at home.** Missing musicians are replenished, the
  first city greets you with three musicians, and formation slots and walking
  routes stay inside the hangar base. Previously displaced members may walk back.
  Original instrument behavior and ten-second formation changes remain.
- **Visual fixes and GPU diagnostics.** Construction-lot surfaces no longer
  overlap, taser shock follows pedestrian poses, and remote sight effects remain
  visible. The package includes a separate Development diagnostic launcher with
  DRED/Aftermath support. The recurring null-address GPU compute fault is still
  unresolved; this release does not claim to fix that crash.

The cage, tasers, helpers and expanded recovery systems are deliberate remake
additions. Earlier radio, flyover, movie, NPC identity, traffic and flight work
remains included. See [all changes](../ForkChanges.md), [controls](../ControllerSupport.md)
and [capture cage details](../CaptureCage.md).

## Download and run

Extract `SimCopterRemake-v1.0.8-Windows-x64.zip` completely, then run the root
`SimCopterRemake.exe`. Keep its accompanying folders together. Unreal Editor is
not needed. The bundled `Engine/Extras/Redist/en-us/vc_redist.x64.exe` supplies the
Visual C++ runtime if Windows requests it.

The package includes 45 maps, 21 KINV tracks, two drive-in movies and 30 city-tour
soundtracks. `Launch-With-GPU-Diagnostics.cmd` is optional; use the normal launcher
for ordinary play. Personal saves/settings, logs, crash dumps and PDB symbols are
excluded. `BUILD_INFO.json` identifies the source commit and executable; the
separate `SHA256SUMS.txt` verifies the ZIP.
