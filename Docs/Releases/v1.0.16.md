## Changes

- **Smoother fast travel:** pedestrian and vehicle creation is spread across frames,
  deferred actors receive their appearance before initialization, and cow poses reuse
  geometry without losing triangles or markings. Baseball teams finish assembling
  within the same population budget.
- **Mouse look and NPC animation:** mouse displacement is independent of frame time;
  keyboard and controller look retain time-based rates. Cached NPC animation sections
  avoid repeated geometry work when switching between idle and walking.
- **Radio:** restored the original music/DJ/commercial/jingle cycle and shuffle rules,
  added per-station Shuffle/Sequential controls, retained KINV's album-order default,
  and kept playback and radio controls available on foot.
- **Medical crew and recovery:** medical missions reserve space for a medic; manually
  dropped medics wait before reboarding except after their final patient handoff when
  there is sufficient room. Fatal pilot accidents return to the surviving helicopter
  after five seconds, or use airport recovery if the aircraft was destroyed.
- **Sky and scenery:** larger sun and moon discs, detailed lunar shading, six park
  layouts with occasional ponds, and paved foundations replace the repeated yellow
  grid. Scene light intensity remains unchanged.
- **Loading and camera presentation:** corrected helicopter camera obstruction feedback
  and startup settling, hidden introductory waypoints, restored time after midday city
  tours, and fixed paused-tour smoke/beacons and oversized beacon cards.
- **Gameplay and controls:** optional cockpit decorations, persistent R3 equipment
  selection, repeated low-fuel warnings, extinguishable road debris, valid fire markers,
  fall-only pilot injury, purchased remote taser access, and user-city/cheat Apache unlock.
  Population coverage includes all 16 installed user maps.
