# SimCopter Remake v1.0.9 — baseball, city life and audio update

This Windows release includes all completed local changes since v1.0.8: full
baseball games, a fixed stadium scoreboard, restored ambient reactions, police
and boat recovery improvements, and the drive-in music volume correction.

## What's new

- **Full nearby baseball games.** Two nine-player rosters play nine innings with
  batting order, balls, strikes, outs, walks, hits, base running, scoring, extra
  innings and walk-offs. Only active players are instantiated: 10–13 per field,
  with at most two nearby games. Visitor uniforms are red and home uniforms blue.
  A fixed green stadium scoreboard has grounded supports, painted labels and
  amber bulb displays for scores, inning, counts, current play and the winner.
  Games restart after their final display or when revisiting a retired field.
  Injured players retain their rescue and save behavior. These complete games
  and the scoreboard are deliberate remake additions.
- **City reactions and aircraft curiosity.** Original casualty-gawking behavior
  is restored, with hospital, carried and hidden casualties excluded. NPC aircraft
  queries use stable parked/owned aircraft identity. Small groups of existing
  civilians can gather around parked helicopters using original animations and
  Simlish, with rotor/door clearance, cooldowns and hazard/mission priority. The
  general helicopter gathering is a remake addition.
- **Police handoffs and medical assistance.** Posted rooftop officers approach
  and arrest landed suspects released onto their own police roof, crediting
  delivery once. Deployed aerial officers can retrieve medical patients when no
  criminal work is available; the last cabin seat goes to the patient.
- **Safer airport placement and burglar traffic.** Newly placed helicopters face
  the airport center, leaving their tails and rear camera corridors away from
  the hangar. Existing saved transforms remain intact. Fleeing traffic retains
  shared braking, and freely driving burglars participate in overlap recovery
  while genuine stops, held cargo and disabled vehicles remain protected.
- **Megaphone reactions and boat delivery.** Pedestrian commands reach the
  original behavior attributes, including restoration of pending saved reactions.
  Eligible cars and illuminated speeders respond to Stop Criminal; it does not
  automatically arrest them, and the original burglar override remains. Boat
  drops use actual hull overlap and short-drop height at harbor rings or police
  roofs. Accepted deliveries seat the hull upright and award recovery once.
  Agent save version 12 continues reading earlier versions.
- **Drive-in music follows the sound settings.** Theaters previously forced 65%
  gain and ignored Music Volume, Game Volume and Auto-Quiet. At 135% music, 100%
  game volume and Auto-Quiet off, gain is now 1.35 instead of 0.65—about 6.35 dB
  higher. Music mute, live changes, pause, distance falloff, movie switching and
  demolition are covered by actual mixer tests. The media files are unchanged.
- **Performance diagnostics alongside crash evidence.** The optional performance
  launcher records CPU/GPU/frame timings together with DRED/Aftermath crash
  diagnostics. The witness-photo slowdown and recurring GPU compute fault remain
  unresolved; this release does not claim to fix either issue.

Earlier hangar, capture cage, fleet, controller, radio and rescue updates remain
included. See [baseball](../BaseballGames.md), [ambient behavior](../AmbientBehaviorRestoration.md),
[police and traffic](../PolicePatientAirportAndTrafficFixes.md),
[megaphone and boats](../MegaphoneAndBoatRecoveryFixes.md),

## Download and run

Extract `SimCopterRemake-v1.0.9-Windows-x64.zip` completely, then run the root
`SimCopterRemake.exe`. Keep its accompanying folders together. Unreal Editor is
not required. If Windows requests the Visual C++ runtime, run
`Engine/Extras/Redist/en-us/vc_redist.x64.exe`.

The package retains 45 maps, 21 KINV tracks, two drive-in movies and 30 city-tour
soundtracks. Normal play uses Shipping. `Launch-With-GPU-Diagnostics.cmd` and
`Launch-With-Performance-Diagnostics.cmd` use the included Development build.
Personal saves/settings, logs, crash captures and PDB symbols are excluded.
`BUILD_INFO.json` identifies the source commit and executable, and the separate
`SHA256SUMS.txt` verifies the ZIP.
