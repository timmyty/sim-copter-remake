# v1.0.23 — misty tours, correctly sized station engines and moving previews

- **Softer tour fog:** reduced local cloud extinction and spread the wisps horizontally. The city stays visible through the mist instead of disappearing behind dense rounded puffs. Each career map keeps its preview-based bank locations; imported maps keep repeatable terrain-based layouts. Metropolis remains clear. Tour completion, skip and teardown still remove the presentation volumes and restore rendering settings.
- **Fire-station engine size:** uniformly reduced both decorative engine noses to 35% of their previous dimensions, retaining their paint, glazing, headlights, trim and shape. Their overall width is now 10.85 GEO units versus the gameplay CARFIRET body’s 10.55, compared with 31 before. Station geometry, bays, roof and gameplay fire trucks are unchanged.
- **Career previews keep moving:** returning to the career picker previously reused cached movie brushes after their players had been closed. Reopen a closed player when its brush is requested again, without restarting a player that is already opening or running. The original rotating map movies remain available with Extra Remake Features disabled, on the first New Career screen and subsequent visits.

The remembered Extra Remake Features toggle after every New/Open selection, Game Options control, pilot-retrieval correction and all earlier fixes remain included. **Game Menu > Options > Gameplay > Emergency Buildings > Previous remake** restores the earlier hospital, police and fire-station styles on the next city load. It works independently of Extra Remake Features and does not revert other landmarks.
