# SimCopter Remake v1.0.20

Emergency headquarters now follow the reference city artwork more closely. Game Options lets you choose the updated buildings or the previous remake models, and User maps stop adding events when three are underway.

## Buildings and options

- **Options → Gameplay → Emergency Buildings:** choose **Updated** or **Previous remake**. The choice is saved and takes effect the next time a city loads. Cancel restores the choice you entered with.
- Hospitals have orange balconies on the entrance-facing section, orange railing around the lower roof terrace, and two separate covered entrance sections. The remake's roof artwork and helipad remain.
- Fire stations return to their original lower height, with a radar dish, two chimney stacks and three fire-engine bays. Police headquarters have stepped blue facades, shields and a broad columned entrance while retaining their remake roofs.
- Previous remake mode restores all three earlier models, including the former taller fire station and hospital facade.
- Marinas gain timber docks, slips, pilings, water beneath the walkways and road approaches while retaining the original house and roof artwork.

## User maps and gameplay

- **At most three ongoing events on User maps**, including background incidents and recovery jobs, at every difficulty and mission-frequency setting. The hangar marker does not count. Older saves already above the limit finish their existing jobs before receiving new ones.
- User-city settings allow manual progression-stage selection and a saved **0.25x–4x mission-frequency** setting. User-city sessions start with Dynamic time.
- Heven and other custom-map traffic no longer stalls because of stale aircraft-probe yaw or mismatched route endpoints.
- Harbor recovery acknowledges delivered boats and explains unsuccessful release attempts. Repeated pursuit messages no longer generate cash, and capture-cage ownership no longer triples crime weighting.
- **Police arrival:** slow police cars now finish their response inside the destination tile, fixing a corner waypoint that could leave them retrying indefinitely.
- Police traffic speeds, rooftop staffing, automatic ambulance limits, medic paths and patient farewells are improved. Band members stop repeatedly spinning.
- The **Respawn helicopter** cheat recalls the active aircraft to the hangar. Below-world recovery, aircraft collision sweeps, fleet recall, turbo descent and airplane reverse taxi are corrected.
- Mission Log scrolling, tour clouds, dock water, building beacon cadence, moon shadow behavior and night-window stability are improved.

These changes include all completed October 18 local updates. Details: [playtest fixes](../PlaytestFixesOctober18.md), [Heven traffic](../HevenTrafficIntersections.md), [marinas](../MarinaDocksOctober18.md), and [artwork options](../EmergencyArtworkOptionsOctober18.md).
